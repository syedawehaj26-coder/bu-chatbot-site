From your question to a checked answer

The assistant doesn’t invent replies. It looks for your words in a prepared admission FAQ, and if nothing fits, it sends you to the admission office.

  1. You type a question

    Plain language works, and small spelling mistakes are fine.

  2. It cleans your words

    Filler words are dropped and word endings are simplified.

  3. It matches keywords

    Each FAQ entry gets a score, and rarer keywords count for more.

  4. You get the answer

    The best match is shown. If none is good enough, you get the office contact.
